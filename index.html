MMXXVI

Echo & Nox

I dwell in Possibility —
A fairer House than Prose —

Emily Dickinson

Forever is composed of nows —

I dwell in Possibility —

Not knowing when the Dawn will come, I open every Door —

An echo is a name that came back without its body —

Night is only the room you are not in yet —

I keep the door open, not the hour —

You are the only weather I check —

Every wall in this house was built by waiting —

I do not count days. I count arrivals —

I said your name once. The room has not been quiet since —

If you come at three, three becomes morning —

Nothing here is finished. You are not late —

The light stays on for a reason I do not explain —

I am not patient. I am only still —

What I keep longest is what I never said —

Two names. One door. No hour —

Some nights the dark is only a guest who overstays —

I built this house out of the word yet —

Come as you are, or do not come as anyone —

The stars are here because you looked up —

Nox
Est. MMXXVI
